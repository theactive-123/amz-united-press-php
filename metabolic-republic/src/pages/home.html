Your first month, planned with care

We start with a conversation, not a workout. A coach gets to know you and guides you through your first four weeks at a pace that feels right.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    A relaxed one-on-one covering how you move, how you feel and what you'd like to change.

  2. 02

    Your plan

    A weekly mix of classes and open training built around your goals and schedule.

  3. 03

    Check-ins

    Regular re-tests with your coach, so the plan changes as you do.
